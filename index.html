Processing invoices

Every invoice goes through five steps: code it, scan it, log it, turn in the original, and check it was paid. You'll use the ThinkPad, the scanner in the office at the Inn, and Ciara's box in that same office. Follow the steps in order.

  1. Code it Write the GL code on the invoice.
  2. Scan it Scanner in the Inn office; save to the COS folder.
  3. Log it One row in the COS Tracker; Balance Check = 0.
  4. Turn it in Original to Ciara's box in the Inn office; she sends it to AP.
  5. Check it was paid Match ProfitSword to the tracker.

Checking in ProfitSword: Site Reports → Monthly Reports → P & L → gear icon → Report View 624 - Restaurant 6 → click the blue amount on the GL line. Ledger Detail lists vendor, invoice #, amount and date posted.

  • Matches → color the row Paid
  • Not ours or not in the tracker → Melissa Barta
  • Wrong GL code → Calvin or Brock

Casey logs food invoices (Cheney as CBI, City Produce); the manager logs Alsco and beverage.

Before you start: ThinkPad basics

  • File Explorer is where all the shared folders are. To open it, press the Windows key and E at the same time, or click the yellow folder icon on the bar at the bottom of the screen.

  • The H drive is the shared drive. In File Explorer, click This PC in the left column, then double-click WC.Common (\\CORDS02) (H:).

  • Double-click a folder or file to open it. Click the back arrow at the top left to go back one folder.

  • To save an Excel file, press Ctrl and S at the same time.

Step 1: Code it

Find the vendor in the table above and write the GL code on the invoice. If one invoice covers more than one code, write each code with its amount. For example, a Cheney invoice with Bloody Mary mix: the food under 55010 and the Bloody Mary mix under 55021.

Step 2: Scan it

  1. Take the invoice to the scanner in the office at the Inn and scan it.

  2. Email the scan to yourself from the scanner. It shows up in your Outlook from Hospitality Scanner.

  3. On the ThinkPad, open File Explorer and go to: This PC → WC.Common (\\CORDS02) (H:) → ACCOUNTING FOOD AND BEVERAGE → 2026 COS Tracker → Scratch.

  4. Double-click the month folder (for example, 10. October), then the numbered folder for that invoice from the table above (for example, 1 Food for Cheney).

  5. Save the scan in that folder.

  6. Keep the original paper invoice. It goes to Ciara in step 4.

Step 3: Log it in the COS Tracker

Casey logs the food invoices; you log the rest (see Who logs what, below).

  1. In the same Scratch folder from step 2, double-click that month's tracker file, for example 10. October 2026 COS Tracker - Scratch. It opens in Excel.

  2. At the bottom of the screen, click the COS Tracker tab. (The other tabs are Summary, budget vs. spent, and Color Key.)

  3. Scroll down to the first empty row and type the Date (MM.DD.YY), Vendor, Invoice # and Invoice Amount. Cheney is typed as CBI.

  4. In the same row, type the amount again in the column for its GL code. If it covers more than one code, split it across those columns. Example: a $1,131.58 Cheney invoice was $1,082.31 under Food (55010) and $49.27 under Wine (55021).

  5. Look at the Balance Check column at the far right. It must say 0. If it doesn't, the split doesn't add up to the invoice amount.

  6. Credits are typed as negative numbers (for example, -39.30). Transfers between outlets use the vendor name "XFER - AP".

  7. Color the row to show its status. Click the row number at the far left to select the whole row. On the Home tab at the top, click the arrow next to the paint bucket (Fill Color) and pick the color. The Color Key tab shows which color means Paid, Scanned Copy in Folder, Unpaid, Transfer, Accrual or Missing.

  8. Press Ctrl and S to save.

Step 4: Turn in the original

Put the original paper invoice (with the GL code written on it) in Ciara Diaz's box in the office at the Inn. Ciara is the Administrative Coordinator (ciara.diaz@stjoe.com). She sends it to AP to be paid.

  • Month end: invoices are due in Ciara's box by 2 PM Monday, and accruals by 2 PM Wednesday. She emails the exact dates each month.

Step 5: Check it was paid in ProfitSword

After AP pays an invoice, it shows up in ProfitSword. Check it against the COS Tracker.

  1. Open Chrome and go to ProfitSword. Log in.

  2. In the left menu, click Site Reports (the house icon at the top). A menu of reports opens.

  3. Under Monthly Reports, click P & L.

  4. At the top of the report, click the gear icon next to "WaterColor Resort".

  5. In the Report View box, choose 624 - Restaurant 6 (that's Scratch). Click OK at the bottom right.

  6. Scroll to the line you're checking (for example, COST OF FOOD-SCRATCH) and click its blue number. The left side of the report is this month; the right side is the year to date.

  7. A Ledger Detail tab opens. It lists every invoice paid on that line: vendor, invoice #, amount and date.

  8. Compare each line to the COS Tracker:

    • It matches: change that row's color to Paid (step 3, item 7) and save.

    • It isn't in the tracker, or isn't Scratch's: question it with Melissa Barta, AP Supervisor.

    • It has the wrong GL code: contact Calvin Whittington or Brock Lucchese.

Processing tips

Tips are paid through the weekly PAS (Pay Adjustment Spreadsheet). It's due to payroll by 10:00 AM every Monday.

Jeremy wants it finished by noon on Sunday, the day before it's due, and he asks for a text when it's done.

Hours from Paycom + tips from Oracle = each person's share (Scratch Master workbook, one day tab at a time, Saturday to Friday)

  • Paycom (Time Management → Timecard Search): open each person and use that day's Hours. Arrows at the top switch to the next person.
  • Oracle (Revenue Tip Center report): use that day's totals. Direct tips → CC Tips; service charges → SC Gratuity.
  1. Type the date at the top (cell A1).
  2. Hours: each person's hours for that day, from Paycom.
  3. CC Tips and SC Gratuity: the day's totals from Oracle, in the top row.
  4. Type Oracle's totals in the Micros row (top right); Variance should read 0.

TTL CC, TTL SVC and TTL Takehome fill in automatically, so don't type over them. Do all seven days; the Weekly Summary tab totals each person's week, then copy those totals into the PAS.

You only type the hours and the day's tip totals. The sheet splits the tips by hours and builds the week for you.

  1. Friday: Payroll (Nancy Otaño or Whitney Yeager) emails "Pay Adjustment Spreadsheet & SOP" with a new PAS file attached (for example, PAS 10.30.2026.xlsm). Always use the newest sheet; it has the latest new hires.

  2. Fill in Scratch Master for each day: hours from Paycom timecards, tips from the Revenue Tip Center report in Oracle Restaurants.

  3. Fill in the PAS for each employee who earned tips that week. Employees with no tips don't need to be added.

  4. Save it as "Scratch PAS MM.DD.YYYY" in the 00 Tips folder:

    • File Explorer → This PC → WC.Common (\\CORDS02) (H:) → Food & Beverage → Scratch → 00 Tips

  5. By noon Sunday, email it to payadjustments@stjoe.com with the subject "Scratch PAS MM.DD.YYYY".

  6. Text Jeremy that the PAS is done.

If an employee isn't on the sheet or you have a coding question, email Nancy Otaño, Sr. Payroll & Benefits Specialist (nancy.otano@stjoe.com). For tip calculation questions, go to Calvin Whittington.

How to prepare

  6. Ask Chef how many biscuits to bake for the contest, plus the raffle pies and muffins.

Alice tickets

Alice is where repair and maintenance requests go so Facilities can track them. Facilities watches it: Dewayne picked up the booth-molding ticket the same day.

What goes in Alice

  • Repairs and maintenance: broken trim or furniture, leaks, water damage, AC problems, equipment that needs Facilities

  • Logbook entries and adjustments (Alice has a logbook)

How to put in a ticket

  1. Open Alice and sign in with your resort account.

  2. Create a new ticket and describe the problem: what it is, where it is, and what caused it if you know.

  3. Attach a photo.

  4. If it's urgent or could hide bigger damage (like a leak), also email Dewayne Singletary and Matt Moore, cc Jeremy, saying you've put in the Alice ticket.

  5. Locked out of Alice? Email Help@joe.com.

Example: on 9/30/2026 I logged broken shoe molding at the booth seating from the AC leak and asked for a mold check before it was replaced. Dewayne replied the same day.
